A small test object: resolution bars (top) + graded circles (bottom).
Source size (focal spot) 1.2 mm
Smaller spot → sharper edges, but fewer photons → darker & noisier. Larger spot → brighter & cleaner, but geometric blur creeps in.
Source-to-sample distance 45 cm
Detector stays fixed at 100 cm. Moving the sample closer to the source → higher magnification, but the penumbra grows so the image also blurs.
Exposure / dose (tube current × time, mAs) 8.0 mAs
More photons → brighter & cleaner (noise falls as 1/√photons) — but every photon is dose delivered to the sample. Too much and the image burns out. Doesn't affect sharpness or magnification.
